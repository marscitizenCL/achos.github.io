Privacy Policy

Achos is built on a simple principle: your music and your data belong to you. This policy explains exactly what we collect — and what we don't.

Effective date: June 2026 · Version 1.0

The short version: Achos runs entirely on your Mac. We do not collect your personal data, we do not track your listening habits, and we do not sell or share your information with third parties. The only external network calls Achos makes are those you explicitly initiate.

01

Who We Are

Achos is developed and published by Martín Gallardo Arenas, an independent software developer based in Santiago, Chile ("we," "us," or "our"). The Achos macOS application ("App") is available on the Mac App Store.

For any privacy-related questions, contact us at: support@achos.app

02

Information We Do Not Collect

Achos is a local-first application. We do not collect, store, or transmit:

  • Your music files or metadata about your library
  • Your listening history, playback habits, or preferences
  • Your name, email address, or any personal identifier
  • Device identifiers, IP addresses, or location data
  • Analytics or crash data beyond what Apple's standard App Store mechanisms provide

All analysis performed by Achos — spectral analysis, loudness measurement, format verification — is computed entirely on your device and is never transmitted to our servers.

03

Third-Party Services You May Connect

Achos integrates with the following third-party services. These integrations are entirely optional and are only activated when you explicitly choose to use them.

Google Gemini AI (AI Filemaster & GRaiL features)

When you use the AI Filemaster or GRaiL features, Achos sends audio spectral data and file metadata to the Google Gemini API to generate AI analysis and musical narratives. This data is processed according to Google's Privacy Policy. You control when these features are invoked — they are never triggered automatically. You may provide your own Gemini API key, which is stored locally on your device using macOS Keychain.

Discogs (Collection Integration)

If you connect your Discogs account, Achos uses OAuth 1.0a to authenticate on your behalf. Achos accesses only your collection data (releases, pressings) as needed to display Pressing Intelligence and Vinyl Oracle features. We do not store your Discogs credentials — authentication tokens are stored locally in macOS Keychain. Your use of Discogs is also governed by the Discogs Privacy Policy.

Apple App Store

Purchasing Achos through the Mac App Store is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy. Apple may collect purchase and usage data per their standard practices. We receive no personally identifiable information from Apple beyond aggregated sales statistics.

04

Data Storage and Security

All data generated or used by Achos is stored locally on your Mac:

  • Application preferences are stored in macOS user defaults
  • API keys and authentication tokens are stored in macOS Keychain
  • Spectral analysis results and AI verdicts may be cached locally on your device
  • No data is transmitted to our servers; we operate no server-side storage

You can delete all Achos-related data at any time by uninstalling the application and clearing its associated data through macOS System Settings.

05

Children's Privacy

Achos is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, please contact us and we will take appropriate steps to remove such information.

06

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Effective date" at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically. Continued use of Achos after changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
